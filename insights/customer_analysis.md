## 🎯 Executive Summary

Our customer base shows strong performance among early adopters (2022 cohort) but declining retention in recent acquisitions. VIP customers drive disproportionate value, while 35% of our customer base is at risk of churning. Key opportunity: Re-engagement campaigns for inactive customers and improved onboarding for new acquisitions.

**Key Metrics:**
- **Average Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** $1,847
- **Overall Retention Rate:** 58%
- **Churn Rate (90+ days):** 35%
- **Repeat Purchase Rate:** 42%
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** $486

2. **Onboarding Gap:** No systematic welcome/education sequence for new customers
3. **Competitive Pressure:** More competitors in 2023-2024 vs. 2022
4. **Product-Market Fit:** Early adopters had stronger need; newer customers more casual

**Recommendations:**

**Immediate (Next 30 Days):**
- Launch 2024 cohort rescue campaign
- Implement automated onboarding email sequence
- Offer 2nd-purchase discount for new customers

**Short-term (Next 90 Days):**
- Revise customer acquisition strategy (focus on quality over quantity)

- Create customer success touchpoints at days 7, 14, 30

**Long-term (6+ Months):**
- Build referral program (leverage strong 2022 cohort)

- Create community features (forums, loyalty programs)

## 🚨 Churn Analysis - At-Risk Customers

### Churn Definition:
Customers with no purchase activity in 90+ days who were previously active (2+ prior orders)

### Current Status:
- **Total At-Risk Customers:** 35 (35% of active base)
- **Potential Revenue Loss:** $87,000 (based on historical LTV)
- **Win-back Success Rate Assumption:** 30% (industry standard)
- **Recoverable Revenue:** $26,100

### At-Risk Breakdown by Segment:

| Segment | At Risk | % of Segment | Avg Prior LTV |
|---------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| VIP | 3 | 11% | $3,200 |
| Regular | 22 | 42% | $1,100 |
| New | 10 | 50% | $400 |

### Key Observations:

**VIP Churn (Low but Critical):**
- Only 3 VIP customers at risk but each worth $3,200
- **High-value recovery opportunity:** $9,600 potential
- Likely reasons: Life changes, competitive offers, service issues
- **Action:** Personal outreach from account manager

**Regular Churn (Largest Group):**
- 22 customers representing bulk of churn risk
- Likely reasons: Price sensitivity, alternatives found, low engagement
- **Action:** Discount-driven win-back + product recommendations

**New Customer Churn (Highest Rate):**
- 50% of new customers never return after first purchase
- **Critical issue:** Onboarding failure or wrong customer acquisition
- **Action:** First-purchase experience audit + onboarding redesign

### Win-Back Campaign Strategy:

**Tier 1: VIP Customers (3 customers)**
- Personal email/call from leadership
- Exclusive "we miss you" 25% discount
- Free premium shipping for 6 months
- VIP concierge service offer

**Tier 2: Regular Customers (22 customers)**
- Segmented email based on last purchase category
- 20% win-back discount
- "New products you might like" recommendations
- Limited-time urgency messaging

**Tier 3: New Customers (10 customers)**
- "Complete your setup" messaging
- 15% second-purchase discount
- Educational content on product benefits
- Lower barrier offer (free shipping, easy returns)

### Expected Results:
- **VIP recovery:** 2/3 customers = $6,400 recovered revenue
- **Regular recovery:** 7/22 customers = $7,700 recovered revenue
- **New recovery:** 3/10 customers = $1,200 recovered revenue
- **Total Expected:** $15,300 recovered revenue (58% of recoverable)

## 🛍️ Purchase Behavior Patterns

### Shopping Day Analysis:

**Most Active Days:**
1. **Tuesday:** 18% of orders (highest)
2. **Wednesday:** 16% of orders
3. **Thursday:** 15% of orders
4. **Sunday:** 14% of orders

**Least Active Days:**
5. Monday: 13%
6. Friday: 12%
7. Saturday: 12%

**Insights:**
- Mid-week shopping dominance (Tue-Thu = 49% of orders)
- Weekend shopping lower than expected (26% combined)
- Monday shows post-weekend recovery

**Marketing Implications:**
✅ Schedule email campaigns for Monday evening (opened Tuesday morning)  
✅ Flash sales on Tuesday-Wednesday for maximum engagement  
✅ Weekend campaigns focused on browsing/cart-building (convert Monday-Tuesday)  
✅ Avoid Friday campaigns (weekend distraction)  

### Time-to-Repeat-Purchase:

**By Customer Segment:**
- VIP customers: 38 days average between orders
- Regular customers: 67 days average
- New customers: 142 days (if they return at all)

**By Product Category:**
- Laptops: 180 days (low frequency, high consideration)
- Smartphones: 365+ days (replacement cycle)
- Accessories: 45 days (frequent replenishment)
- Office novelties: 30 days (gift purchases, impulse)

**Strategic Applications:**
- VIP: Monthly engagement cadence
- Regular: Bi-monthly promotional campaigns
- Accessories: Replenishment email at day 40
- High-value products: Long-term nurture campaigns

## 💡 Final Thoughts

Our customer base shows a classic maturity pattern: strong early adopters with declining performance in newer cohorts. The data clearly indicates we need to shift from pure growth to retention and optimization.

**The Good News:**
- We have a solid foundation (2022 cohort at 68% retention)
- VIP segment performing exceptionally well
- Clear, actionable insights on where to intervene

**The Urgent Issues:**
- 35% of customers at churn risk
- New customer retention at only 25%
- Each successive cohort performing worse than previous

**The Opportunity:**
With focused retention efforts, we can recover $15K+ in short term and build foundation for sustainable growth. The Office-themed products provide unique brand differentiation and community building opportunities we're not fully leveraging.
